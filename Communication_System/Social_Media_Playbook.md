# Social Media Playbook

Platform-agnostic principles for organic viral content.

---

## Core Principle

Write content people want to send in a private message to a friend. That's the algorithm signal that matters across all platforms.

---

## The Private Share Test

Before posting, ask: "Who would DM this to someone specific?"

Content that passes:
- Solutions to problems friends are facing
- Validation of shared struggles
- Humor that feels personal
- "I know who needs to see this" content

Content that fails:
- Generic announcements
- Feature lists
- Corporate voice
- No emotional hook

---

## Emotional Triggers That Drive Shares

**Top 10 (in order):**
1. Amusement
2. Interest
3. Surprise
4. Happiness
5. Delight
6. Pleasure
7. Joy
8. Hope
9. Affection
10. Excitement

**Key finding:** Surprise + Positive emotion = maximum sharing

**Research stats:**
- Content with strong emotional hooks gets 8x more shares
- Surprise is most consistently present in viral content

---

## Post Length

**Sweet spot: 40-80 characters**

- Posts under 50 characters perform 86% better
- Longer posts get cut off with "See More" — every click loses audience
- Exception: Longer works IF hook succeeds in first 10 words

**Structure:**
1. Hook (first 5-10 words) — most important
2. Body — delivers value
3. CTA — creates engagement

---

## What Actually Drives Virality

1. **Novelty** — genuinely new/unexpected
2. **Emotion** — strong feelings (joy, surprise, awe)
3. **Relatability** — universal experiences
4. **Social value** — content worth sharing to show who you are
5. **Utility** — practical value to pass along
6. **Timing** — right moment, right cultural conversation

---

## Authenticity Beats Polish

Research consistently shows:
- Raw posts outperform polished production
- User-generated content beats professional content
- Authentic personality wins over corporate voice
- Imperfect feels human; perfect feels fake

---

## Small Pages Can Win

2025 algorithm reality:
- 500 followers have same viral potential as 5 million
- Follower count matters less than content quality
- Interest-based distribution (like TikTok)
- 50% of content comes from "unconnected sources"

What small brands do:
- Find unique angles only they can show
- Enable easy sharing (GIFs, simple formats)
- Tap emotions over promotions
- Ride cultural moments
- Stay consistent

---

## Timing Guidelines

**General windows:**
- Peak: 1-3 PM weekdays
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Peak posts get 32% more engagement

**Note:** Timing matters less than content quality. A great post at a bad time beats a bad post at a good time.

---

## Platform-Specific Notes

### Facebook
- Color background posts stand out
- Native content gets 2-3x more reach than links
- Don't delete underperformers — can gain traction later
- Groups have better reach than pages

### Twitter/X
- Threads outperform single tweets for depth
- Quote tweets drive conversation
- Timing matters more here

### Instagram
- Visual-first always
- Carousels outperform single images
- Stories for ephemeral, feed for permanent

### LinkedIn
- Professional but human
- Authentic stories outperform thought leadership
- Comments matter more than likes

## Content Categories

| Type | Purpose | Emotion Target |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Behind-the-scenes | Connection | Interest, Surprise |
| Educational | Value | Utility, Hope |
| Relatable/Humor | Engagement | Amusement, Joy |
| Inspirational | Shares | Hope, Affection |
| Engagement (polls) | Comments | Interest |
| Feature spotlight | Awareness | Delight, Surprise |

---

## Red Flags (Content That Fails)

- Corporate voice / press release tone
- Feature lists without emotion
- "We're excited to announce..."
- No actor in the story (ghost writing)
- Closing the loop too early (no curiosity)
- Over-explaining
- Multiple messages in one post

---

## Pre-Post Checklist

1. [ ] Hook in first 5-10 words?
2. [ ] Would I stop scrolling?
3. [ ] Is there an actor doing something?
4. [ ] Strong emotion present?
5. [ ] Would someone DM this?
6. [ ] Under 80 characters (or hook justifies length)?
7. [ ] Sounds human, not corporate?
